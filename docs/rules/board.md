# Card-Cheesi — The Board

## Layout

The board consists of **64 positions** arranged in a loop, plus **4 finish positions** per player.

### Home Positions

Each player has a designated **home position** where their pawns enter the board:

| Position | Player |
|----------|--------|
| 1        | Player 1 home |
| 17       | Player 2 home |
| 33       | Player 3 home |
| 49       | Player 4 home |

Between each home position there are **15 regular positions**, evenly spacing the 4 players around the board.

## Finish Area

- After completing a full loop of the board, a pawn approaches its **finish area**: 4 consecutive finish positions that only pawns of that color may enter.
- A pawn must advance around the entire board and then **walk into** the finish positions.
- Pawns in the finish area **can never leave** the finish area.
- A pawn in a finish position is **protected** — no other pawn may pass or jump over it (see [Pawn Rules](pawns.md)).
- Players should fill finish positions in order (closest first) so later pawns can still enter.

# Card-Cheesi — Game Overview

Card-Cheesi is a board game for **4 players**, inspired by Parcheesi. Instead of rolling dice to determine movement, players draw and play cards.

## Players and Teams

- There are always exactly **4 players**.
- Players compete in **2 teams of 2**:
  - **Team A**: Player 1 and Player 3
  - **Team B**: Player 2 and Player 4
- Each player has **4 pawns** of their own color.

## Winning the Game

- A player advances their own pawns until all 4 are in the finish area.
- Once a player's own 4 pawns are all finished, that player may move their **teammate's** pawns.
- The game ends when **both players of a team** have all their pawns finished. That team wins.

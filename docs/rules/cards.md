# Card-Cheesi — Cards

Each turn a player plays one card from their hand and executes its effect. Below are all card types and their rules.

## Special Cards

### Ace
The player may choose **one** of the following:
- Bring one pawn from their reserve **into play** at their home position.
- Advance a pawn already in play **1 position**.

### King
- Bring one pawn from their reserve **into play** at their home position.

### Four
- **Reverse** one pawn already in play by **4 positions** (move backward).

### Seven
The player may choose **one** of the following:
- Move **one pawn** 7 positions forward.
- **Split** the 7 positions across **at most two pawns** (e.g., 3 + 4, 1 + 6, etc.). Both partial moves must be valid.

### Jack
- **Swap** the board positions of **two pawns**.
- The two pawns **must be of different colors**.
- The player may choose any two pawns on the board, including pawns from the opposing team.
- See [Pawn Rules — Swapping](pawns.md#swapping-pawns) for restrictions on protected pawns.

## Numbered Cards

All remaining cards move a pawn **forward** by the number of positions equal to the card's value:

| Card  | Positions moved |
|-------|-----------------|
| 2     | 2               |
| 3     | 3               |
| 5     | 5               |
| 6     | 6               |
| 8     | 8               |
| 9     | 9               |
| 10    | 10              |
| Queen | 12              |

> A numbered card can only be used to advance a pawn that is **already in play**.

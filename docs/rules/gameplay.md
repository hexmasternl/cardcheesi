# Card-Cheesi — Gameplay

## Dealing Cards

Cards are dealt in **3 rounds** per dealer turn.

| Round | Cards dealt per player |
|-------|------------------------|
| 1     | 5 cards                |
| 2     | 4 cards                |
| 3     | 4 cards                |

One player is designated the **dealer** for all three rounds. After all three rounds are played, the dealer position advances clockwise to the next player.

## Turn Order

- The player **to the left of the dealer** starts each round.
- Play proceeds clockwise.
- Each player takes a turn, then the next player goes, and so on, until all cards have been played or disposed.
- Once all players have exhausted their cards, the next round begins and new cards are drawn.

## Taking a Turn

On their turn, a player must either:

1. **Play a card** — choose a valid card from their hand and execute its action (see [Cards](cards.md)).
2. **Dispose all cards** — if no valid move is possible with any card in hand, the player discards their entire hand and skips their turn.

## Playing Teammate Pawns

- A player plays **only their own pawns** until all 4 of their pawns are in the finish area.
- Once a player's own pawns are all finished, they may play their **teammate's** pawns for the remainder of the game.

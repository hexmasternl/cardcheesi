# Card-Cheesi — Pawn Rules

## Bringing Pawns Into Play

A pawn enters the board at the player's **home position** by playing an **Ace** or a **King**.

When a pawn is brought into play, it is immediately **protected** (see below).

## Protected Pawns

A pawn is **protected** when it is first placed on its home position.

Rules for protected pawns:
- Other players **cannot hit** (capture) a protected pawn.
- Other players **cannot pass** a protected pawn — not even a teammate.
- Only the player who **owns** the pawn may move a protected pawn.
- The pawn **loses its protection** as soon as it is moved or its position is swapped.

## Hitting a Pawn

When a pawn lands on a position occupied by an unprotected pawn of another player, the occupying pawn is **hit** and returned to its owner's reserve. The hitting pawn takes that position.

## Finish Area

Once a pawn enters the **finish area**, it is protected for the rest of the game:
- No pawn may pass or jump over a pawn in a finish position.
- The owner of the pawn (or the teammate playing on their behalf) may still advance it within the finish area.
- A pawn **cannot leave** the finish area once it has entered.
- Players should place pawns in the finish positions **in order** (closest first) to keep the entry path clear for other pawns.

## Swapping Pawns

When a **Jack** is played, the player swaps the positions of two pawns. The following rules apply:

- The two pawns **must be of different colors**.
- A protected pawn that the controller does not own **cannot be swapped** — this includes a teammate's protected pawn.
- **Proxy mode**: When a player has finished all 4 of their own pawns they begin playing their teammate's pawns. Those teammate pawns become the controller's pawns for the rest of the game, so the teammate's protected pawns may participate in a swap *as the controller-owned side* of the swap.
- Pawns in the **finish area** cannot be swapped.
